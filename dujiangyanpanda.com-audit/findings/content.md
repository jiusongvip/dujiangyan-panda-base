# Content Quality Findings

**Score**: 65/100  
**Category Weight**: 23%

## Issues Found

### [Critical] Blog page has no real content
The /blog/ page lists 4 blog post titles with dates but all links go to #. Google will see this as a soft 404 or thin content page.

**Recommendation**: Either publish real blog posts immediately or remove the listing page.

### [High] About page is dangerously thin
~150 words across 3 generic paragraphs. No author bios, credentials, or contact info. Major E-E-A-T weakness for a travel information site.

**Recommendation**: Expand to 500+ words with author/team info, credentials, and editorial methodology.

### [High] No author or content attribution
No bylines or editorial credentials appear anywhere. Google's E-E-A-T guidelines heavily weight author transparency.

**Recommendation**: Add author schema and visible bylines. Even "Editorial Team" is better than nothing.

### [Medium] No content freshness signals
No "last updated" dates on any content page. Travel information changes and Google values temporal signals.

### [Medium] No contact page or contact information
The about page mentions "contact channels" but no contact page exists.

### [Medium] Missing privacy policy and terms pages
Trust signals increasingly expected by search engines.
